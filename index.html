Pallino

A Pomodoro timer that grows while you focus.

  • macOS · in App Store review
  • iPhone & iPad · coming soon
  • Italian · English
  • free

Pick your minutes and press Start. While you work, Pallino builds himself one dot at a time; when he is complete the session is done and he joins your nest. Quit halfway and he runs away. Streaks unlock new friends. It lives in the menu bar, and on iPhone in the Dynamic Island.
